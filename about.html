About

I am a computational social scientist working on AI learning and digital inequality, completing an MSc at Linköping University on a Swedish Institute Scholarship.

I spent eight years in IT support and IT leadership at a school, and for four of those years I also taught programming, first at the school and then at an NGO for students from low-income communities. That experience shapes the questions I ask now.

Today I use surveys, social networks and text data to ask who gets access to AI, who trusts it and who learns with it. I program in R and Python and bring training in computer science, systems analysis and development, science and technology, and law. I am looking for PhD positions in Scandinavia starting in 2027.
